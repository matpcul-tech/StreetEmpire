Add a model

  1. Download a .glb or a glTF .zip (Sketchfab: Download 3D Model, glTF).
  2. Rename it to the slot: sedan.glb, house-2.zip.
  3. On GitHub open kit-inbox/cars, kit-inbox/buildings or kit-inbox/characters, then Add file, Upload files, Commit.
  4. In about 3 minutes it shows here and in the game.

Facing the wrong way? Add @yaw180 (or 90, -90) before the extension and upload again. Credit file: same name with .txt.

Characters must be rigged (Meshy: Rigging, Humanoid). Slots: player, unc, crew, reds, blues, golds, locals, cop, ped. Men and women: reds-m-1.glb, reds-f-1.glb. Height: @h1.82. The full list is public/models/kit/character-roster.json.
