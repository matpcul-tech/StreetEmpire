# Street Empire art style guide (v0.4)

One look for the whole game: a gritty early 1990s West Coast city seen through warm, dusty haze. Original work only. No real brands, logos or names from other games.

## Mood

- Late afternoon that never quite ends. Heat haze, smog and a big orange sun.
- Lived-in and worn: faded paint, grime, cracks, bars on windows, rolled-down gates.
- Readable on a phone first. Big shapes and strong silhouettes beat small detail.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Notes |

Overall grade: slightly washed out, lifted blacks, warm highlights, mild teal in deep shadows, light film grain.

## Textures

- Photo-based and painted, never clean or flat. Every surface shows wear: water stains, rust drips, cracks, sun fade.
- Tileable surfaces (brick, stucco, cinderblock, asphalt, sidewalk, glass tower, corrugated metal) are made seamless.
- Hero facades (liquor store, corner market, house, roll-down gate, graffiti) are single painted images placed whole on a wall.
- Phone budget: 512 px for tiling surfaces, 1024 x 512 for facades, WebP, under about 80 KB each.
- Signs use made-up businesses only: LIQUOR, CORNER MARKET, COLD DRINKS, LOTTO.

## Shapes

- Eastside: low one storey houses with porches, flat or low pitched roofs, chain-link yards, palm trees, telephone poles with sagging wires, a basketball court, corner stores and liquor stores.
- Downtown: tall boxy 70s and 80s office towers with window grids, walled apartment blocks with AC units.
- Harbor: wide warehouses in corrugated metal, shipping containers, cranes, water.
- Props are chunky and slightly oversized so they read at phone scale. Corners stay hard and boxy.
- Cars are 90s and boxy: sedans, a lowrider with chrome, SUVs, vans, cruisers. Paint is sun faded with dirt low on the body.
- People: hoodies, jerseys, jeans, caps and bandanas in crew colors. Faces stay simple and readable.

## Light

- The sun follows the day and night cycle and its color drives the sky, fog and reflections.
- Dusk is the signature moment: long shadows, orange rim light, purple shadows.
- Night is lit by sodium lamps, neon signs and windows.
