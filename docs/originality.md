# Originality guardrails

Street Empire is its own game. The open-city crime genre, turf wars, a wanted level, a radar and stealing cars are ideas no one owns, but a specific look that copies one game's expression can be a problem. These rules keep the game clearly its own.

## Never use
- The names Grand Theft Auto, GTA, Rockstar, or any of their place, gang, character, vehicle, radio or business names (Los Santos, San Andreas, Vice City, Liberty City, Grove Street, Ballas, Vagos, CJ, Sweet and so on), in the game, store pages, ads or file names.
- The Pricedown font or anything made to look like it, or GTA-style comic-panel key art.
- A copy of the San Andreas HUD: green zero-padded money counter, five white wanted stars.
- Real gang names, signs or colors as a crew identity.
- Real car brands, badges, grilles copied from one model, or license plates from a real state.
- Real store brands and logos.

## Our identity
- **Crew colors:** player orange, Red Kings red, Blue Hand blue, Gold Line bronze. Not green, purple, yellow or teal (the San Andreas gangs).
- **HUD:** cream money with commas, and a HEAT meter of five slanted bars instead of stars.
- **Art direction:** 90s West Coast, our own districts (Eastside, Downtown, Harbor) and made-up businesses.

## Prompts for art (Meshy and others)
Describe the thing, never a game: "1980s American four-door sedan, generic, no badges" works; "car from GTA San Andreas" does not. The same goes for buildings and characters.

This is a design guide, not legal advice. Before selling the game, a short review with an IP lawyer is cheap insurance.
