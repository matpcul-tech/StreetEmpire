## Text layout
- Left strip under the radar (beside it in landscape): mission line (max 3 lines), onboarding as one step "1/3 ...", capture text, and toasts in portrait.
- Toasts sit in a top band between the radar and the HUD on desktop and landscape.
- Cash popups sit under the cash counter.
- Desktop E prompt is small and low; phones use the TALK / CAR button instead.
- BLOCK CLAIMED, mission titles, WASTED and BUSTED are a short centered chip (1.6 s phone, 2.2 s desktop), smaller on phone, above the player.
- Mission passed card is smaller on phone.
- The objective arrow avoids the radar, the HUD column and the thumb controls.
- Safe area insets on radar, HUD, strip, touch controls. Landscape pause button moved to bottom center.
- Messages shortened (for example "Unc is 18 m away. Follow the UNC arrow.").
- The audit fails if any visible text box overlaps the radar, the stick, the steer or button cluster, or the player and road box (center 28% wide, 38% to 80% tall).

## Crew
- Squad: up to 2 recruits follow you. More recruits go to guard your least guarded block.
- Claiming a block: a new guard spawns and the squad stays behind, up to 3 guards per block, posted around the block edge and slowly patrolling. They fight rivals and cops that come into the block.
- T (desktop) or SQUAD (phone) inside your block: take one guard along, or send your squad to guard it.
- Minimap shows the guard count on each of your blocks; block info toast shows "N guards".
- Car: when you get in, the squad runs to the car and hops in (at most 1.2 s, 4 seats), rides along hidden, and hops out beside the car with you.
- Pace: squad runs up to 11 m/s (sprint is 9), and snaps back in when 40 m behind or stuck for 2.5 s.

## Night readability without the post grade
- Potato (and the low fps fallback) have no grade pass, so characters read as silhouettes. Fix: the existing shader night rim and clothing lift get a 1.9x gain and exposure +0.26 at night when there is no composer. No extra lights.
