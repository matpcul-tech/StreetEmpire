# Playtest v0.10

Source: three bot playtests (first 30 seconds, direction, HUD, touch, performance, visuals).
Tools: tools/v010-shots.mjs (new), tools/playtest.mjs, tools/smoke.mjs, tools/visual-qa.mjs, npm run test:missions.
Screenshots: /workspace/screenshots/v0.10-*.png and /workspace/screenshots/v0.10-qa/.

## Fixes
1. Hook: a neutral Local walks up about 9 m from spawn at 2.5 s and starts a scuffle. He never counts for captures.
2. Direction: a 3 step onboarding panel (knock out the Local, talk to Unc, take a block), a labelled arrow (LOCAL, UNC, RING), a desktop "E Talk to Unc / Get in" prompt and an E message when nothing is in reach.
3. Minimap: 200 px (136 px phone), streets, blocks, rings, Unc marker, pulsing target.
4. Stars: off stars are outlines and the shown level holds 0.5 s, so no 0/3/2 flicker.
5. Title shows the real version (v0.10 plus quality).
6. Portrait title: card sits under the art, larger high contrast control text, a click while LOADING starts the game when ready.
7. Touch: touch class on first touch, stick drag moves the player, long press menu, callout and selection off, stale touch ids dropped.
8. Resize: canvas refits on resize and on a 500 ms check.
9. ?q=potato (also low/high), saved choice in the pause menu, automatic pixel ratio drop and potato offer when frames stay over 70 ms.
10. Cash popups name the source (hustle, blocks, mission reward).
11. Cooler dusk fog and hemisphere light, textured green dumpster, matte bags, softer player rim and rougher player material.
12. Pause on Esc or P, II button on phone; sim stops while paused.
