# v0.8.1 playtest

Date: 2026-11-04. Build: local vite preview of the release bundle, then the live Pages site.
Viewports: desktop 1280x720 and phone (iPhone 13 layout, real touch input, Low quality).
Screenshots: /workspace/screenshots/v0.8.1-*.png, /workspace/screenshots/playtest-v0.8.1 and /workspace/screenshots/qa.

## Fixes
1. Dusk sky. A twilight glow dome now follows the sun: a warm band toward the sunset, a blue violet zenith, and a soft city haze at night. Twilight keeps the night fill light. Desktop dusk close up scene mean went from 35 to 80, and every check passes the standard limit of 38 (the lowered close up limit is gone).
2. Phone mission text. The mission line and the dialog are now compact, semi-transparent banners in the top band just under the HUD (140 px and 172 px from the top). The title flash is smaller. The road and the car stay clear while driving.
3. Phone tap combat. Taps made during the attack cooldown are buffered for 0.4 s instead of being dropped. Melee also lunges to a locked target up to 1.6x weapon range, so knockback no longer makes the next hit whiff. Phone combat now goes 60 to 0, the same as desktop.

Visual QA also skips characters hidden behind walls (raycast), which caused a false silhouette fail.

## Still weak
- The dusk sky reads more lavender than orange. A stronger sunset color would be a nice touch.
- On phone, the title flash still crosses the far horizon for about 2 s when a mission starts.
- All of this was measured in headless software rendering. A check on a real phone is still worth doing.
