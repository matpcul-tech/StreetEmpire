# v0.9 playtest: cinematic moments

Date: 2026-11-04. Build: local vite preview of the release bundle, then the live Pages site.
Viewports: desktop 1280x720 and phone (iPhone 13 layout, real touch input, Low quality).
Tools: tools/cine-shots.mjs (new, drives every moment and captures it), tools/playtest.mjs, tools/smoke.mjs, npm run test:missions, tools/visual-qa.mjs.
Screenshots: /workspace/screenshots/v0.9-*.png and /workspace/screenshots/playtest-v0.9.

## What shipped
All moments are presentation only (src/cine.js). The state layer stays instant, so the sim, mission tests and rules do not change. The one exception is a 1.1 s down beat before respawn. Any tap, click, Space, Enter, Escape or E skips the current moment.

1. Car entry and exit. The player walks to the driver door, a painted door panel swings open, the player ducks in and sits, then the door closes. Meanwhile the camera eases from behind the player to behind the car. Entry takes 1.15 s and exit 0.95 s (the reverse). Jacking adds a 0.4 s beat at the start: the player reaches in (punch cross clip) and the driver staggers out to the curb (hit clip). Nothing gory. Driving input is held during the move.
2. Unc briefings. There is one per mission, 3 or 4 lines and about 6 to 8 s long. Letterbox bars slide in and the HUD hides. The first cut is over the player's shoulder, then every line cuts to whoever is speaking (a three quarter medium close up). There is a slow push in on each cut. Unc plays the UAL Idle_Talking_Loop and the player plays it on "You" lines. Lines show in a styled subtitle box. The sim is paused, so mission timers do not run. Each cut raycasts the real scene to pick a camera spot with no wall or prop in the way. No lip sync, no voice.
3. Small beats.
   - Block claim: a 1.7 s camera swing around the block, then "BLOCK CLAIMED".
   - Wasted or busted: the sim runs at 0.4x for the 1.1 s down beat (about 2.75 s real time), with a red (wasted) or blue (busted) vignette and the camera pulling up and back. Busted holds hands up instead of falling.
   - Mission passed: a card with the title, cash and respect for 3.2 s, skippable.

## Fixed during the playtest
- Starting a new moment while one was playing left the letterbox and hidden HUD stuck. The current moment now ends first.
- Briefing cameras landed inside walls in tight spots. Each cut now raycasts for a clear spot from four candidates.
- Car exit never triggered because of an undefined down timer check.
- The mission title flash overlapped the passed card. The card now clears it.

## Still weak
- The door is a simple painted panel added beside the body. The Kenney cars have no separate door mesh, so at some angles it reads as a slab rather than part of the car.
- Duck in and sit is a blend of clips plus a body lean. There is no dedicated enter car clip in the trimmed UAL set, so the move is quick and readable but not fluid.
- Briefings run 6 to 8 s. They are skippable, but repeat players will probably tap past them.
- In very tight alleys the over the shoulder opener can fall back to a side angle that loses the shoulder.
- All timings come from headless software rendering. A look on a real phone is still worth doing.
