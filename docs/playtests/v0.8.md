## Lighting
Night and dusk now pass visual QA on both viewports. Characters at night measure 55 to 97 mean luma (QA floor 40), so they no longer read as black silhouettes.
Dusk used to be darker than full night (scene mean about 21). Twilight now uses the night fill, and the dusk scene mean is 35 to 62.

## Still weak
- At dusk the sky goes nearly black near 18:00 instead of showing a sunset glow. The staged close up at dusk has a scene mean of 35, which passes only because close ups have their own floor of 30. The characters in that shot are well lit.
- On phone, the mission title and dialog text sit over the middle of the driving view for a few seconds.
- Combat on the phone needs more taps per hit than on desktop.
- All numbers come from headless software rendering. A check on a real phone is still worth doing.
