STREET EMPIRE

You are a youngster on the corner of Eastside with one block to your name. Three crews run the rest of the neighborhood, and they want yours too.

Beat a block's defenders, then hold its zone to take it. Every block runs a hustle that pays you over time. Spend cash on recruits who follow and fight for you. Steal a car to get around fast.

Win: own all 9 blocks. Lose: lose all your turf.

Desktop: WASD move, mouse look (click to lock), Shift sprint, Click or F punch, E enter or exit car, R recruit.
Phone: left stick moves, drag right side to look, buttons for the rest.
